Dispatch ranking prototype

The rules in 00-rook/code/dispatch-routing/ as something you can play with: score, rank, ask one at a time, update the yes-history score.

Example people, not Rook data. Responders A to D and every starting number are made up so you can see how the rules behave. Change anything.

1 · The people

Four people are free in the area. Set how far each is, how often they have said yes lately, whether they have the skills, and how they behave when asked.

2 · The ranking

Score = weight × proximity + weight × yes-history + weight × skills. Best first. Nobody is removed.

3 · Send one callout

Asks down the list. An answer later than the ping wait is a miss. A miss and a turn-down both take 0.12 off; a take adds 0.08. The yes-history sliders above update, so you can watch the ranking change.

4 · Send 20 callouts in a row

Starts from the numbers above (it does not change them). Shows what happens to everyone's yes-history score over time.

Things to try: set A's answer time to 75 seconds and compare a 90-second wait with a 60-second wait in section 4. Then set the weights to "Before 4.2" and see whether A's slide changes. Not modelled: who is marked available, how travel time is estimated, and any rule outside the files in the routing folder. Proximity is 1 minus travel minutes ÷ 45, and 0 past 45 minutes.
