# Customer interviews: complaints by area

Source: rook-wiki, Customer interviews database (console redesign research, 2-5 Sep 2026). Four one-on-one calls, all with handlers, all run by the product designer.

Urgency is my judgment from what the handlers said about impact. The interviews do not rate anything. Count is how many of the four handlers raised the complaint.

## Who was interviewed

| Handler | Responder(s) | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Mr. Ambrose | Captain Vantage | 2 Sep |
| Aunt Dot | Vesper | 3 Sep |
| Kip | Meteor Mite, The Gale | 4 Sep |
| Halloran | Sgt. Bulwark | 5 Sep |

Halloran is mostly Supply-side and said the console is "fine", so he raised no console complaints. No responders, quartermasters or support staff were interviewed; anything about responders' phones is secondhand.

## Console features (in scope)

| # | Feature | Complaint | Raised by | Count | Urgency | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alerts | Live callout easy to miss | Dot, Ambrose | 2 of 4 | High | Ambrose tied it to losing jobs |
| 2 | Text and visuals | Text and status badge too small | Dot, Ambrose | 2 of 4 | Medium | Can't read status at a glance |
| 3 | Filters | Reset silently, no warning | Ambrose | 1 of 4 | Medium | Worked 15 minutes on the wrong filter |
| 4 | Alerts | Same sound for every responder | Kip | 1 of 4 | Low-Medium | Only affects handlers with several responders |
| 5 | Text and visuals | No dark mode | Kip | 1 of 4 | Low-Medium | Comfort, but his one ask |
| 6 | Coverage cards | Can't tell why two cards differ | Kip | 1 of 4 | Low-Medium | He couldn't say what he'd want |
| 7 | Coverage view | Too much clicking to confirm status | Ambrose | 1 of 4 | Low | He doubted a fix exists |
| 8 | Detail panel | Tag legend buried in a drawer | Ambrose | 1 of 4 | Low | Used about once a fortnight, "not urgent" |

Note on alerts: the asks differ. Ambrose wants the live signal harder to miss, Dot wants to be told herself (she called the current chime "a good chime"), Kip wants a different sound per responder.

## One line per complaint, in the handlers' words

- **Live callout easy to miss** (Ambrose): "whatever tells me a callout is live to be harder to miss, not easier."
- **Live callout easy to miss** (Dot): "maybe something that tells me too, not just him, when something's come in, so I'm not relying on hearing a buzz through a ceiling."
- **Text too small** (Ambrose): "at a glance I sometimes cannot tell engaged from available without leaning in."
- **Text too small** (Dot): "leaning right in with wet hands over a laptop is not something I recommend to anyone."
- **Filters reset** (Ambrose): "I'd rather it warned me the filter had reset than simply reset it."
- **Same alert sound** (Kip): "Mite and Gale both just go 'bing' and I have to actually look to know which one."
- **No dark mode** (Kip): "the console is just this wall of white light in the middle of my face."
- **Cards tell different stories** (Kip): "I'm looking at two cards on the same screen that might as well be two different products."
- **Clicking through** (Ambrose): "More than I'd like, but I don't know that there's a fix for it that doesn't clutter the view for everyone else."
- **Tag legend** (Ambrose): "It sits in a drawer off the responder detail panel and I open it perhaps once a fortnight."
- **Callout gone** (Dot): "by the time he's actually got a thumb on the screen — it's gone. Gone to somebody else already."
- **Callout gone** (Ambrose): "coat on, one boot on — when it went to somebody else."
- **Uneven workload** (Kip): "Meteor Mite's been texting me all week asking if something's broken because it's so quiet… And The Gale hasn't stopped, like, at all, same week, same city."
- **Requisitions** (Halloran): "One queue, everything in it, doesn't matter if it's laces or plate."
- **Failure reports** (Halloran): "I file a failure report and it goes into a void."
- **Catalog search** (Halloran): typed "plate" and got forty results, half of them unrelated.

## What works, per the handlers

- Filter persistence, when it holds (Ambrose)
- The chime sound (Dot)
- Two coverage cards side by side (Kip)
- The layout overall (Kip)
- Maintenance scheduling no longer books into busy stretches (Halloran)

## Caveats

- Four handlers is a small sample, and one barely uses the console.
- Count is how many people raised a complaint, not severity. The loudest complaint, the requisition queue, has a count of 1.
